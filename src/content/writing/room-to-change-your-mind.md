---
title: Give teams room to change their minds
summary: A small change to the way teams write down decisions.
label: Article
order: 2
---

A decision record is more useful when it gives someone a way to revisit the decision. Try ending it with two or three things that would change your mind.

Six months later, the next team can check whether those things happened. They have a starting point for the conversation and a record of what you knew at the time.
