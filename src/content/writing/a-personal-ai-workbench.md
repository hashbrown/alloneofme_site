title: A personal AI workbench
summary: Start by noticing the jobs you do over and over.
label: Series · Part 1
order: 3
---

Before looking for an assistant, spend a few days noticing what you repeatedly collect, compare, reorganize, or forget.

Pick one small job. Write down how long it takes today and what a good result looks like. Then you have something concrete to compare after a week.
