summary: What makes a tool stay in your workflow after the novelty wears off? A question for anyone building with personal AI.

order: 1
---

I’ve been thinking about which tools earn a place on a personal AI workbench. A demo can make almost anything feel indispensable for ten minutes. The useful question is what I still reach for after two weeks.

Try giving a tool a recurring job and writing down each time you use it without a reminder. If you keep going back to your old workflow, look at what makes the old way easier. That may tell you more than another feature comparison.
